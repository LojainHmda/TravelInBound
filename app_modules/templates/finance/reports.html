About Financial Reports
Profit & Loss Statement

The Profit & Loss Statement provides a comprehensive overview of your agency's financial performance by showing:

  • Total revenue from client bookings
  • Expenses categorized by type
  • Supplier costs for travel services
  • Gross profit (revenue minus supplier costs)
  • Net profit (gross profit minus operational expenses)

Use this report to assess your overall financial health and track profitability trends over time.

Expense Summary

The Expense Summary breaks down all operational expenses by category, helping you:

  • Identify your highest cost areas
  • Track expense trends over time
  • Compare expenses across different periods
  • Find potential areas for cost reduction

This report is essential for budgeting and cost control measures.

Revenue Summary

The Revenue Summary analyzes your income streams by service type, providing insights into:

  • Which services generate the most revenue
  • Revenue patterns across different periods
  • Seasonal trends in bookings and income

Use this report to focus your marketing efforts on the most profitable services.
