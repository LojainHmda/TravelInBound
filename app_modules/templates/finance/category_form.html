Category Guidelines
Best Practices for Expense Categories

When creating expense categories:

  • Use clear, descriptive names that reflect the nature of the expenses
  • Keep category codes short but meaningful for easy reference
  • Create specific categories rather than general ones for better reporting
  • Consider your reporting needs when designing your category structure
  • Be consistent in how you categorize similar expenses

Common expense categories for travel agencies include:

  • Office Expenses: Rent, utilities, supplies, maintenance
  • Personnel: Salaries, benefits, training, travel
  • Marketing: Advertising, promotions, website, social media
  • Professional Services: Legal, accounting, consulting
  • Technology: Software subscriptions, hardware, IT services
  • Insurance: Business insurance, liability coverage
  • Taxes & Fees: License fees, permits, business taxes
