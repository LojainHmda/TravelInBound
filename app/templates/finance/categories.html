About Expense Categories

Expense categories help you organize and track your operational expenses. Using consistent categories makes it easier to:

  • Generate accurate reports on expense distribution
  • Track spending patterns across different areas of your business
  • Identify areas where costs might be reduced
  • Prepare accurate financial statements and tax reports

Each expense must be assigned to a category, so make sure to create categories that cover all aspects of your business operations.
