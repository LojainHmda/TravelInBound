About Statements

Supplier statements of account provide a summary of the financial transactions between your company and a supplier over a specific period.

Use these statements to:

  • Track outstanding balances
  • Verify payment histories
  • Reconcile accounts
  • Plan future payments

The statement includes all service confirmations and associated costs along with any payments made to the supplier.

Tips
  • Use date filters to focus on specific periods
  • Filter by payment status to identify unpaid items
  • Print statements for your records
  • Record payments directly from the statement view
